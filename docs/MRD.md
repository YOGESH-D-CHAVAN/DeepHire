# Market Requirements Document (MRD) - DeepHire

## Executive Summary

DeepHire is an AI-powered interview platform designed to revolutionize the hiring and interview preparation process. It leverages cutting-edge AI and computer vision technologies to provide candidates with realistic interview experiences and employers with intelligent candidate evaluation tools.

## Market Overview

### Problem Statement

**Current Hiring Challenges:**

1. **Interview Preparation Gap**: Candidates struggle to find realistic interview practice environments with quality feedback
2. **Interviewer Inconsistency**: Traditional interviews lack standardization, leading to subjective evaluations
3. **Time Investment**: Recruiters spend significant time on initial screening and interview coordination
4. **Candidate Anxiety**: Lack of practice environments increases interview anxiety and poor performance
5. **Scalability Issues**: Manual interview processes don't scale efficiently for high-volume hiring

### Target Market

**Primary Users:**

- **Job Seekers** (18-50 years old): Professionals preparing for interviews at tech companies, consulting firms, and Fortune 500 companies
- **Recruiters & HR Teams**: Companies of 50-5000+ employees looking to streamline hiring
- **Career Development Platforms**: Educational institutions and bootcamps

**Secondary Users:**

- **Executive Coaches**: Career professionals offering interview coaching services
- **Large Enterprises**: Organizations with high-volume hiring needs

### Market Size & Opportunity

**Total Addressable Market (TAM):**

- Global recruitment software market: $15B+ (2024)
- Interview preparation market: $2-3B annually
- Initial focus: North American tech recruiting (high-intent users)

**Growth Drivers:**

- Remote work normalization increasing demand for virtual interviews
- AI adoption in HR tech accelerating
- Shift toward candidate-centric hiring experiences
- Post-pandemic emphasis on interview efficiency

## Product Vision

Create the **most effective AI-powered behavioral interview platform** that replicates real HR interview scenarios and helps candidates master the STAR method, while enabling organizations to conduct consistent, competency-based hiring through:

- Realistic HR interviewer simulations with authentic questioning patterns
- STAR method coaching and evaluation
- Behavioral competency assessment (leadership, teamwork, communication, problem-solving)
- Real-time facial expression and stress response analysis
- Company-specific interview pattern matching

## Key Features & Value Propositions

### For Candidates

1. **Authentic Behavioral Interview Simulation**
   - HR interviewer avatars with realistic questioning patterns and follow-ups
   - STAR method training and real-time guidance
   - Stress interview scenarios to build confidence
   - Company-specific interview question banks (Google, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, etc.)
   - Adaptive difficulty based on role level (entry, mid, senior)

2. **Competency-Based Feedback**
   - Leadership & influence assessment
   - Teamwork & collaboration evaluation
   - Problem-solving & analytical thinking
   - Communication clarity and structure
   - Stress management and composure tracking
   - Detailed STAR compliance scoring

3. **Comprehensive Practice**
   - 50+ behavioral interview scenarios per role
   - Follow-up questioning practice (realistic probing)
   - Multiple attempt tracking with improvement metrics
   - Peer comparison and benchmarking
   - Interview transcript analysis with specific suggestions

### For Employers

1. **Consistent Behavioral Screening**
   - Standardized competency-based interviews
   - Automated STAR method compliance checking
   - Structured evaluation rubrics
   - Candidate ranking by competency fit
   - Reduced interview bias through standardization

2. **Authentic Assessment**
   - Real-world behavioral scenarios from your company
   - Follow-up question libraries for deeper probing
   - Stress interview capabilities for senior roles
   - Cultural fit evaluation
   - Video recordings with emotion tracking

3. **HR Analytics & Insights**
   - Competency heatmaps by candidate
   - Hiring pipeline analytics
   - Interview consistency metrics
   - STAR method compliance reporting
   - Hiring speed optimization

## Competitive Landscape

### Direct Competitors

- **InterviewBit**: Coding-focused, limited soft skills evaluation
- **LeetCode Interview**: Similar coding focus, limited behavioral assessment
- **Pramp**: Peer-to-peer, lacks AI personalization
- **Codewars**: Community-driven, limited interview preparationg

### Competitive Advantages

1. **Behavioral Interview Specialization**: Purpose-built for HR interviews, not generic Q&A
2. **STAR Method Integration**: Built-in STAR structure detection and coaching
3. **Realistic HR Patterns**: AI learns from real HR interviewer transcripts for authentic follow-up questions
4. **Competency Framework**: Maps responses to SHRM and industry competency models
5. **Company-Specific Libraries**: Pre-built interview patterns for FAANG and Fortune 500
6. **Advanced Stress Testing**: Realistic pressure scenarios and push-back questions
7. **Authenticity**: Real-time emotional authenticity analysis (not just smiling detection)

## Business Model

### Revenue Streams

**B2C (Individual Users)**

- **Freemium Model**: 1-2 free interviews, limited features
- **Pro Subscription**: $9.99-14.99/month or $99/year
- **Premium Plus**: $19.99-24.99/month with unlimited interviews + advanced analytics

**B2B (Enterprise)**

- **Team Licenses**: $500-2000/month (10-50 users)
- **Enterprise Plans**: Custom pricing based on volume and features
- **Integration Fees**: API access for ATS integration

### Pricing Strategy

- **Value-based**: Premium pricing based on superior AI and analysis quality
- **Usage-based**: Enterprise customers pay based on interview volume
- **Subscription-focused**: Recurring revenue model for predictability

## Risk Assessment

### Technical Risks

- AI accuracy in non-English scenarios
- Real-time performance at scale
- Privacy concerns with facial recognition

### Market Risks

- User privacy regulation changes (GDPR, CCPA)
- Competitor innovation
- Market adoption slower than projected

### Operational Risks

- Talent acquisition for AI/ML expertise
- Sustaining growth with limited funding
- Enterprise sales cycle complexity
